## Refund Policy

### Standard Refunds
- Customers may return products within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund
- Items must be in original condition with all packaging and accessories
- Refunds are processed within **5–7 business days** after receiving the returned item
- Shipping costs for returns are the customer's responsibility unless the return is due to a defect or error on our part

### Defective / Incorrect Items
- Full refund or replacement offered at no cost to the customer
- Prepaid return label provided
- No restocking fee

### Non-Refundable Items
- Digital downloads and software licenses
- NovaProtect+ plans (after 30-day cancellation window)
- Products with removed serial numbers

### How to Initiate a Refund
1. Go to your account at account.novatech.example.com
2. Select "Orders" and click "Return Item"
3. Choose a return reason and submit
4. Print the prepaid shipping label (for eligible returns) or use your own

### Partial Refunds
- Opened accessories returned after 15 days: 50% restocking fee applied
- Products returned without original packaging: 10% deduction
