# Warranty Policy

## Overview
All NovaTech products come with a **limited manufacturer warranty** that covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use conditions.

## Standard Warranty Coverage
- **Consumer Electronics (Headphones, Speakers, Streaming Devices):** 1 year from date of purchase
- **Tablets and Computing Devices:** 2 years from date of purchase
- **Wearables (Smartwatches, Fitness Bands):** 1 year from date of purchase
- **Accessories (Hubs, Cables, Adapters):** 90 days from date of purchase

## What Is Covered
- Manufacturing defects
- Hardware malfunctions not caused by misuse
- Battery defects within 6 months of purchase (capacity below 80% of original)
- Screen defects (dead pixels, backlight failure) not caused by physical damage

## What Is NOT Covered
- Physical damage (cracked screens, bent frames, liquid damage)
- Damage caused by unauthorized modifications or repairs
- Normal wear and tear (scratched casings, faded buttons)
- Damage due to misuse, accidents, or failure to follow product instructions
- Products with removed or altered serial numbers

## How to Claim Warranty
1. Contact NovaTech support at support@novatech.example.com or via live chat
2. Provide proof of purchase (order confirmation or receipt)
3. Our team will assess the issue remotely or arrange shipping for inspection
4. If approved, we will repair or replace the product at no charge

## Extended Warranty (NovaProtect+)
- Available for purchase within 30 days of original purchase
- Extends coverage to 3 years
- Includes accidental damage protection (1 claim per year)
- Available for $29.99/year for standard devices, $49.99/year for tablets

## Important Notes
- Warranty is non-transferable and applies only to original purchaser
- Replacement units carry the remaining warranty of the original device or 90 days, whichever is longer
