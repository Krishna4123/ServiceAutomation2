# Subscription Plans & Billing

## Available Plans

### Basic Plan — $9.99/month
- Access to NovaTech cloud storage (10 GB)
- Standard customer support (email, 48-hour response)
- 1 linked device
- Monthly billing

### Premium Plan — $19.99/month
- Access to NovaTech cloud storage (100 GB)
- Priority customer support (live chat + email, 4-hour response)
- Up to 5 linked devices
- Access to exclusive member discounts (10% off hardware)
- Monthly or annual billing ($199/year — save ~17%)

### Enterprise Plan — Custom Pricing
- Unlimited cloud storage
- Dedicated account manager
- 24/7 phone + email support with 1-hour SLA
- Unlimited linked devices
- Volume hardware discounts (15–30%)
- Custom onboarding and API integrations
- Annual billing only (contact sales@novatech.example.com)

## Plan Management

### Upgrading Your Plan
- Upgrades take effect immediately
- You'll be charged a prorated amount for the remaining billing period

### Downgrading Your Plan
- Downgrade takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle
- No penalty for downgrading

### Cancelling a Subscription
- You may cancel at any time through your account settings
- Access continues until the end of the current billing period
- No partial refunds for unused time (unless within 7 days of renewal — full refund issued)

## Billing FAQ

**When am I billed?**  
On the same date each month (or year, if on annual billing) from the date you subscribed.

**What happens if payment fails?**  
We retry payment 3 times over 7 days. If unsuccessful, your account downgrades to a free tier and cloud sync pauses.

**How do I update my payment method?**  
Go to Account → Billing → Payment Methods.

**Can I pause my subscription?**  
Subscriptions can be paused for up to 3 months once per calendar year. Contact support to initiate a pause.

**Are there family or student discounts?**  
- Family Plan (up to 6 members): $29.99/month (Premium features)
- Student discount: 50% off Premium with valid .edu email
