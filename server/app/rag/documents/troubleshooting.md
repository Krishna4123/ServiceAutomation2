# Troubleshooting Guide

## NovaPro Wireless Headphones

### Headphones Won't Power On
1. Ensure the headphones are charged — connect via USB-C for at least 30 minutes
2. Hold the power button for 5 seconds (not 2)
3. Reset: hold both volume buttons + power simultaneously for 10 seconds

### Bluetooth Pairing Issues
1. Delete the headphones from your device's Bluetooth list
2. Put headphones in pairing mode (hold power 8 seconds until LED flashes red/blue)
3. Re-pair from your device settings
4. Ensure no more than 2 devices are simultaneously connected

### Poor Sound Quality / Distortion
- Move closer to the connected device (within 10 meters)
- Avoid interference from microwaves, Wi-Fi routers, or other Bluetooth devices
- Reset equalizer settings in the NovaTech app to defaults
- If one ear is silent: clean the audio port and check for firmware updates

## AuraTab 10 Inch Tablet

### Tablet Won't Start
1. Charge for 20 minutes before attempting to power on
2. Hold power + volume down for 15 seconds to force restart
3. If boot screen loops: perform a factory reset from Recovery Mode (power + vol up at boot)

### Wi-Fi Connectivity Issues
1. Toggle Wi-Fi off and back on
2. Forget the network and reconnect
3. Restart your router
4. Check that the router supports 2.4 GHz (the AuraTab does not support 5 GHz on Basic firmware)
5. Update tablet firmware: Settings → System → Software Update

### Touchscreen Unresponsive
- Wipe screen with a clean, dry cloth
- Remove screen protectors that may interfere
- Perform a soft reset (hold power 10 seconds)
- Factory reset if issue persists after soft reset

## ZenStream 4K Streaming Stick

### No Signal / Black Screen
1. Unplug and re-insert the streaming stick into the HDMI port
2. Switch to the correct HDMI input on your TV
3. Try a different HDMI port
4. Ensure the USB power cable is connected (requires 5V/1A minimum)

### Buffering / Poor Streaming Quality
- Check your internet speed (minimum 25 Mbps for 4K)
- Move the streaming stick closer to your Wi-Fi router or use a Wi-Fi extender
- Clear the app cache: Settings → Apps → [App Name] → Clear Cache
- Restart the streaming stick from Settings → System → Restart

## CloudSync Smart Speaker

### Speaker Not Responding to Voice Commands
1. Check that the microphone is not muted (physical mute button on top)
2. Ensure the device is connected to Wi-Fi
3. Re-run the setup: hold the reset button for 10 seconds
4. Check microphone sensitivity in the NovaTech app

### Poor Audio Quality
- Ensure the speaker is not obstructed or placed in a corner
- Clean the speaker grille with a soft brush
- Reduce bass if distortion occurs at high volumes
- Factory reset and reconfigure if issue persists

## PulseWatch Gen 3

### Watch Not Syncing with Phone App
1. Ensure Bluetooth is enabled on your phone
2. Open the NovaTech Health app and tap "Sync Now"
3. Forget and re-pair the watch in the app
4. Restart both the watch and phone

### Inaccurate Heart Rate Readings
- Wear the watch snugly, 1–2 finger widths above the wrist bone
- Clean the optical sensor on the back of the watch
- Stay still for 30 seconds during measurement
- Avoid measuring immediately after vigorous exercise

### Battery Draining Quickly
- Disable always-on display
- Reduce GPS polling frequency
- Turn off "Continuous Heart Rate Monitoring" in settings
- Update firmware to the latest version

## General Support Escalation Triggers
If any of the following apply, escalate immediately to a human agent:
- Device physically damaged or has liquid damage
- Customer has already attempted all troubleshooting steps without resolution
- Issue involves data loss
- Device is within warranty and requires repair or replacement approval
